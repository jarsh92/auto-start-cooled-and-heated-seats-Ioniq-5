AUTO SEAT 2.0 - autoseat.heat - AI-WRITTEN ENHANCEMENTS
No independent human code review. Josh Waldmire directed the AI work and tested his car.
Original HKMC Cold-start Heat credit: Gregory Taylor (eclipseempire).

1. If an older Auto Seat is installed, uninstall it through NEMESIS My Packages.
   Do not just uninstall its Android icon. The installer never uninstalls automatically.
2. Extract ALL CONTENTS of the USB ZIP directly to an otherwise empty USB.
   NEMESIS.data and NEMESIS.sh must be at root. Leave the inner ZIP compressed.
3. Park, let Android fully start, insert USB and approve Execute. Do not interrupt power/USB.
4. Return Auto-Seat-Clean-Install.log if it stops; keep all files unchanged before review.
   Additional Android error details are saved automatically on installation failure.

The visible name is Auto Seat. The current NEMESIS ID/service is autoseat.heat / autoseat.heat/apply.
The original signed app/Android ID stays unchanged. A coldstart.heat helper directory is required
by its fixed path; that helper is not a second registered package or service.
Settings may remain; missing values get defaults. Reset them in the app if desired.
No settings migration/restore or old HOLD workflow is used. Old backups are not deleted.

Owner-tested: US 2023 IONIQ 5 Limited Gen5W, eng.vmadmin.20260903.161833, NEMESIS 1.16.7.
Other cars, trims, firmware and IONIQ 6 are unverified/not supported by this release.
Do not bypass the build guard. Hardware detection/trim toggles are planned, not included.
