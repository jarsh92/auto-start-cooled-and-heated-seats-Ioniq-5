AUTO SEAT 2.0 — COMPATIBILITY: US ONLY, NOT EU

INTENDED TARGET
US-market 2023 Hyundai Ioniq 5 with Android-based Gen5W head unit,
NEMESIS already installed, and exact internal Android build:
  eng.vmadmin.20260903.161833
The owner reported Auto Seat 2.0 works and looks great on this target car
on November 13, 2026. Other vehicles/firmware remain unverified.

NOT A UNIVERSAL IONIQ 5 INSTALLER
Other US model years/trims and even another car with the same internal build
are unverified. EU/European, Canadian, other non-US markets, ccNC head units,
and other Hyundai/Kia/Genesis models are not supported by this package.
Setting the dashboard to Fahrenheit or Celsius does not change its region.
Features require the corresponding heated/ventilated seats and heated-wheel
hardware. The app cannot add hardware missing from a trim.

SOFTWARE VERSION CHECK
The identifier above is ro.build.version.incremental, an internal Android
build ID, not the full retail Hyundai software version shown in Settings.
Its September 3, 2026 date alone does not establish compatibility.
Record the full software/model screen, vehicle year, trim and US region when
requesting review of another car.

The installer refuses a different internal build. It does not separately
verify region, year, trim, equipment or every framework file. Passing its
build check alone is not proof of compatibility with another vehicle.
If installation reports Unsupported head-unit build, stop; do not bypass
the check or install different firmware to force it.

This package does not install NEMESIS and does not update Hyundai firmware.
See README.md for the compatibility table, installation and test steps.
